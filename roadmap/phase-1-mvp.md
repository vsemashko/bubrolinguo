## Phase Overview

**Goal:** Launch functional Polish learning app with core features to validate product-market fit

**Target Launch Date:** End of Month 6

### Key Deliverables
- 50 structured lessons (A1-A2)
- 1,000 vocabulary words with audio and images
- Web application (Next.js PWA)
- iOS mobile app (React Native)
- 2 characters (Zofia, Ania)
- Basic gamification (XP, streaks, achievements)
- User authentication and progress tracking

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 10,000 registered users by end of Month 6
- [ ] 40%+ 7-day retention rate
- [ ] 70%+ lesson completion rate
- [ ] 4.0+ app store rating
- [ ] 500 paying users (early adopters)
