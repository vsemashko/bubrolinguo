**Version:** 1.0

## Product Vision

### Vision Statement
Bubrolinguo will democratize advanced Polish language learning by combining proven pedagogical methods with cutting-edge AI technology, enabling anyone to achieve conversational fluency and C1-level proficiency through an engaging, personalized, and comprehensive learning experience.

### Mission
To break down language barriers between Polish and the Russian/English-speaking world by creating the most effective, accessible, and enjoyable Polish learning platform.

## Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors

#### 1. Duolingo Polish Course
**Strengths:**
- Massive brand recognition
- Free tier with large user base
- Gamification and engagement
- Well-designed UX

**Weaknesses:**
- Stops at A2 level (basic)
- Limited vocabulary depth
- No exam preparation
- Generic error feedback
- No conversational AI

**Our Advantage:**
- Extended proficiency path (C1)
- 10K vocabulary vs. ~2K
- Exam preparation modules
- AI-powered explanations and conversation
- Polish-specific optimizations

#### 2. Babbel Polish
**Strengths:**
- Structured curriculum
- Grammar explanations
- Speech recognition
- Professional content

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited to B1 level
- Expensive subscription
- No mobile app optimization
- No AI features
- Smaller vocabulary

**Our Advantage:**
- More affordable pricing
- C1-level content
- AI conversation practice
- Better mobile experience
- Spaced repetition for vocabulary

#### 3. Memrise Polish
**Strengths:**
- Video with native speakers
- Strong vocabulary focus
- Community-created content

**Weaknesses:**
- Inconsistent content quality
- Limited structure
- No exam prep
- Vocabulary-only focus
- Outdated interface

**Our Advantage:**
- Professional, validated content
- Structured progression
- Comprehensive learning (not just vocabulary)
- Modern, engaging interface

#### 4. Private Tutors (iTalki, Preply)
**Strengths:**
- Personalized human interaction
- Real conversation practice
- Cultural insights
- Flexible topics

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive ($15-30/hour)
- Requires scheduling
- Quality varies widely
- No structured curriculum
- Limited vocabulary tools

**Our Advantage:**
- Always available AI conversation
- Structured curriculum + conversation
- Fraction of the cost
- Consistent quality
- Integrated vocabulary and grammar

### Indirect Competitors

- **General Language Apps**: Busuu, Mondly, Rosetta Stone
- **Flashcard Apps**: Anki, Quizlet (vocabulary only)
- **YouTube/Free Content**: Inconsistent, no structure
- **University Courses**: Expensive, time-bound, location-dependent

## Product Scope

### In Scope - MVP (Phase 1)

**Learning Content:**
- A1-A2 level structured lessons (40-60 lessons)
- 1,000 core vocabulary words with audio
- Basic grammar explanations
- 5 exercise types (translation, multiple choice, listening, speaking, fill-in-blank)

**Features:**
- User registration and authentication
- Progress tracking and analytics
- XP and streak system
- Basic spaced repetition for vocabulary
- 2-3 character introductions

**Platforms:**
- Web application (responsive)
- iOS mobile app (native or React Native)

**Languages:**
- Interface: Russian, English
- Learning: Polish

**Technical:**
- Cloud hosting (AWS/GCP)
- TTS for vocabulary words
- Basic STT for pronunciation
- Content management system

### In Scope - Phase 2

**Learning Content:**
- B1 level lessons (60-80 lessons)
- Expand to 5,000 vocabulary words
- Topical vocabulary modules (20+ topics)
- AI mistake explanations
- Grammar deep-dives

**Features:**
- Complete spaced repetition system
- Topic-based learning mode
- Basic AI conversation scenarios
- 5-6 full character roster
- Achievement system
- Leaderboards

**Platforms:**
- Enhanced web app (PWA with offline)
- Improved iOS app
- Basic analytics dashboard

### In Scope - Phase 3

**Learning Content:**
- B2-C1 level lessons (100+ lessons)
- Complete 10,000 word vocabulary
- Exam preparation modules (B1/B2/C1)
- Advanced conversation scenarios
- Cultural content

**Features:**
- Free-form AI conversation
- Weak area identification and practice
- Mock exams
- Writing practice with AI feedback
- Social features (friends, groups)

**Platforms:**
- Android app
- Advanced analytics
- Admin dashboard for content management

### Explicitly Out of Scope

**Not Planned:**
- Live human tutoring (may partner instead)
- Video content creation (cost-prohibitive)
- Learning languages other than Polish (Phase 1-3)
- Group video classes
- Certificate issuance (we prep for exams, not certify)
- Children-focused content (<13 years old)
- Offline-first mobile experience (MVP)

**Future Consideration:**
- B2B enterprise offerings
- Integration with official exam registration
- Community content creation
- Additional source/target languages
- Browser extensions
- Smart TV/wearable apps

## Constraints & Assumptions

**Resource Constraints:**
- Initial team: 1-2 developers, 1 designer, 1 content creator
- Budget: Limited funding (bootstrapped or seed round)
- Timeline: 6-month MVP target

**Technical Constraints:**
- Must support older mobile devices (iOS 14+)
- Audio files must be optimized for mobile data usage
- AI features limited by API costs and latency

**Content Constraints:**
- Need native Polish speakers for content validation
- Audio recording requires professional quality
- Cultural sensitivity review required
- Licensing for images and media assets

**Regulatory Constraints:**
- GDPR compliance (EU users)
- COPPA compliance if allowing under-13 (currently not in scope)
- App Store and Google Play policies
- Copyright for learning materials

### Assumptions

**Market Assumptions:**
- Polish language learning market will continue growing
- Users willing to pay $5-15/month for quality content
- AI technology will continue improving and becoming more affordable
- Mobile-first learning will remain dominant

**User Assumptions:**
- Users can commit 15-30 minutes per day
- Users have reliable internet connection (initially)
- Users prefer self-paced learning over scheduled classes
- Gamification increases engagement and retention

**Technical Assumptions:**
- Cloud infrastructure costs remain manageable at scale
- AI APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic) remain available and affordable
- TTS/STT technology continues improving
- React Native or similar provides acceptable cross-platform performance

**Business Assumptions:**
- Can achieve 5-8% conversion to paid within 12 months

- Content creation costs can be managed with structured processes
- Can find qualified Polish language experts and validators

## Risks & Mitigation Strategies

### High-Priority Risks

#### 1. Content Quality Risk
**Risk:** Inaccurate, low-quality, or culturally insensitive Polish content damages reputation

**Impact:** High (user trust, learning outcomes)
**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Hire certified Polish language teachers as content reviewers
- Implement 3-stage review process (creation → linguistic review → cultural review)
- User feedback mechanism for reporting errors
- Regular audits of existing content
- Partner with Polish universities or language institutions

#### 2. AI Reliability Risk
**Risk:** AI provides incorrect grammar explanations or inappropriate conversation responses

**Impact:** High (learning outcomes, user safety)
**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Extensive prompt engineering and testing
- Content filtering and moderation
- Human review of AI-generated content samples
- Clear disclaimers about AI limitations
- User feedback and reporting mechanisms
- Fallback to pre-written explanations for common mistakes

#### 3. User Retention Risk
**Risk:** Users drop off before achieving meaningful progress

**Impact:** High (business viability)
**Likelihood:** High (industry average: 70% churn in first week)

**Mitigation:**
- Strong onboarding experience showing immediate value
- Quick wins in first session (learn 10 words, complete 1 lesson)
- Personalized push notifications and email campaigns
- Streak system and loss aversion tactics
- Social proof and community features
- Regular content updates and events

#### 4. Technical Scalability Risk
**Risk:** Platform can't handle user growth, leading to performance issues

**Impact:** High (user experience, reputation)
**Likelihood:** Low (with proper architecture)

**Mitigation:**
- Cloud-native architecture from day one
- Use CDN for all media assets
- Database optimization and caching strategies
- Load testing before major releases
- Monitoring and alerting systems
- Auto-scaling infrastructure

### Medium-Priority Risks

#### 5. Monetization Risk
**Risk:** Unable to achieve target conversion rates or pricing too high/low

**Impact:** High (business viability)
**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- A/B testing of pricing models
- Competitor pricing analysis
- Freemium model to build user base
- Multiple pricing tiers
- Localized pricing for different markets
- Regular user surveys on willingness to pay

#### 6. Competition Risk
**Risk:** Major player (Duolingo, Babbel) launches improved Polish course

**Impact:** Medium (market share)
**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on advanced learners (B1-C1) where competitors are weak
- Build strong community and brand loyalty
- Continuous innovation (AI features)
- Superior content quality
- Faster iteration and feature releases

#### 7. Content Sourcing Risk
**Risk:** Difficulty finding qualified content creators and validators

**Impact:** Medium (development timeline)
**Likelihood:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Build relationships with Polish language departments at universities
- Remote work allows hiring from Poland
- Create clear content creation guidelines and templates
- Build content creation tools to streamline process
- Consider outsourcing to established language content companies

### Low-Priority Risks

#### 8. Platform Policy Risk
**Risk:** Changes to Apple/Google app store policies affect distribution

**Impact:** Medium (distribution)
**Likelihood:** Low

**Mitigation:**
- Maintain web app as alternative distribution
- Stay updated on policy changes
- Ensure compliance with all policies
- Diversify user acquisition channels

#### 9. Third-Party Dependency Risk
**Risk:** Critical services (OpenAI, AWS, TTS providers) experience outages or pricing changes

**Impact:** Medium (features, costs)
**Likelihood:** Low

**Mitigation:**
- Abstract AI integrations to allow switching providers
- Budget buffer for pricing increases
- Redundancy for critical services
- Gradual degradation strategies (fallback to non-AI features)

## Open Questions

### Product Questions
1. Should we allow community-contributed content (like Memrise)? Risk vs. value?
2. What's the optimal lesson length for engagement? 5 min? 10 min? 15 min?
3. Should we offer live group classes or events? Or stay pure app-based?
4. How do we handle Polish regional dialects and variations?
5. Should we create separate tracks for different use cases (travel, business, academic)?

### Business Questions
1. What's the optimal pricing tier structure? 1 tier? 2 tiers? 3 tiers?
2. Should we offer lifetime purchases or subscription-only?
3. Do we pursue B2B opportunities from the start or wait until B2C is proven?
4. Should we seek partnerships with Polish government/cultural organizations?
5. What's the right balance between free and paid content?

### Technical Questions
1. React Native vs. native iOS/Android? What's the right trade-off?
2. Which AI provider offers the best cost/quality balance for our use case?
3. Should we build our own TTS for Polish or use commercial providers?
4. How do we handle offline functionality without excessive app size?
5. What's the right balance between on-device processing and cloud processing?
