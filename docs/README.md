## Key Numbers at a Glance

### Product Scope
- **Lessons:** 250 (A1 to C1)
- **Vocabulary:** 10,000 words
- **Characters:** 6 unique personalities
- **Exercise Types:** 10+ varied types
- **Languages:** Russian & English interface → Polish target

### Development Timeline
- **Phase 1 (MVP):** 6 months → 50 lessons (A1-A2), 1,000 words
- **Phase 2:** 6 months → 110 total lessons (A1-B1), 5,000 words, AI features
- **Phase 3:** 6 months → 250 lessons (A1-C1), 10,000 words, exam prep
- **Phase 4:** 6 months → Scale, optimize, expand
- **Total:** 24 months to full platform

### Investment Required
- **Phase 1:** $480K-580K
- **Phase 2:** $676K-776K
- **Phase 3:** $895K-995K
- **Phase 4:** $1,050K-1,150K
- **Total (24 months):** $3.1M-3.5M

### Revenue Projections
- **Month 6 (Launch):** $5K MRR, 10K users, 500 paid
- **Month 12:** $40K MRR, 50K users, 4,000 paid
- **Month 18:** $150K MRR, 150K users, 15,000 paid
- **Month 24:** $500K MRR, 500K users, 50,000 paid

### Target Metrics
- **Retention:** 40%+ (7-day), 20%+ (30-day)
- **Engagement:** 15+ min sessions, 70%+ completion rate
- **Conversion:** 5% → 10% free-to-paid over time
- **Rating:** 4.5+ stars (app stores)
- **LTV:CAC:** >2:1 (target 3:1)
