## Overview

This document outlines additional features that should be considered for Bubrolinguo beyond the core functionality defined in the main PRD documents. These features enhance engagement, learning outcomes, and platform differentiation.

## Section 1: Enhanced Learning Features

### 1.1 Reading Comprehension Module

**Description:** Dedicated reading practice with authentic Polish texts

**Features:**
- Curated articles at different levels (A1-C1)
- Interactive reading: Click words for instant translation
- Comprehension questions after reading
- Reading speed tracking
- Vocabulary highlighting (known vs. unknown words)

**Content Types:**
- News articles (simplified for levels)
- Short stories
- Blog posts
- Polish literature excerpts (C1)
- Cultural articles

**Value:**
- Builds reading comprehension
- Vocabulary in context
- Exposure to authentic Polish

**Phase:** Phase 3-4
**Estimated Effort:** Medium (content creation)

---

### 1.2 Writing Practice with AI Feedback

**Description:** Free-form writing exercises with AI correction and feedback

**Features:**
- Writing prompts by level and topic
- Text editor with spell-check
- AI analyzes writing for:
  - Grammar errors
  - Vocabulary usage
  - Sentence structure
  - Style and clarity
- Detailed feedback with explanations
- Track writing improvement over time

**Exercise Types:**
- Short answer (50-100 words)
- Essay (300-500 words)
- Email writing (formal/informal)
- Story completion
- Describe an image

**Value:**
- Develops writing skills (often neglected)
- Personalized feedback
- Safe practice environment

**Phase:** Phase 3
**Estimated Effort:** Medium (AI integration)

### 1.5 Grammar Mastery Challenges

**Description:** Focused practice on specific grammar topics

**Features:**
- Select grammar topic (e.g., genitive case)
- 50-100 targeted exercises
- Progressive difficulty
- Mastery test at end
- Badge upon completion

**Grammar Topics:**
- All 7 Polish cases (7 challenges)
- Verb aspects (perfective/imperfective)
- Motion verbs
- Gender and agreement
- Past tense formation
- Future tense
- Conditional mood
- Numbers and declension

**Value:**
- Master difficult grammar
- Focused, intensive practice
- Sense of achievement

**Phase:** Phase 2-3
**Estimated Effort:** Medium (content creation)

---

### 1.6 Pronunciation Clinic

**Description:** Dedicated pronunciation training for challenging Polish sounds

**Features:**
- Focus on difficult sounds: ą, ę, ł, cz, sz, rz
- Minimal pairs practice (kot vs. kod)
- Video showing mouth position
- Record and compare with native speaker
- AI-powered pronunciation scoring
- Repeat until mastered

**Modules:**
- Vowel sounds
- Consonant sounds
- Consonant clusters
- Stress and intonation
- Common mistakes for Russian/English speakers

**Value:**
- Improve pronunciation
- Confidence in speaking
- Address specific challenges

**Phase:** Phase 3
**Estimated Effort:** Medium

---

### 1.7 Immersion Mode

**Description:** Full-immersion experience with interface in Polish

**Features:**
- Switch entire app to Polish interface
- All instructions in Polish
- Help available in native language (toggle)
- Advanced learners can practice reading UI
- Character speaks only Polish

**Value:**
- Advanced learners benefit from immersion
- Practice reading Polish in every interaction
- True language environment

**Phase:** Phase 3
**Estimated Effort:** Low (translation work)
